Our games

Three titles in development, all built around the loops Roblox players come back to every day.

In development

Anime Mage

Click to build your magic power, train at enchanted stones, and hatch pets that boost every gain. Battle through zones for Wins and Gems, rebirth for permanent multipliers, and unlock new characters to transform into.

In development

Nest Raiders

Steal and defend

Slip into a guarded nest, grab the prize, and sprint it home before its guardian catches you. Every prize you bring back earns for you, so defend your own nest from raiders who want it.

In development

Grow a Hero

Summon and climb

Summon heroes at magic gates, put them to work on your plot, and train them up. Then send them up the Tower one floor at a time, and fuse heroes to pass on their skills.

How we build

We're a small team, so we use AI to do the work of a much bigger one. Claude is part of every step, from writing game code to building 3D assets.

  • Claude CodeWrites and reviews our Luau game code, synced into Roblox Studio with Rojo.
  • Claude in Roblox StudioConnected through MCP so Claude can build and test directly in our places.
  • Claude in BlenderDrives our modeling pipeline for characters and props.
